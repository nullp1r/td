# Mara Reed

> **Canonical character specification.** Preserve the recognition anchors below across future art and writing unless the character is deliberately redesigned.

## Identity

- **Name:** Mara Reed
- **Current in-game role:** Harbor Warden at Old Harbor
- **Meta/visual role:** Rustwater's recurring promotional heroine, viewpoint character, **face and mascot**.

Mara is the full identity of the NPC currently presented in-game as **“Mara, Harbor Warden.”**

Her full name and visual design emerged during exploration of Rustwater promotional/anime artwork. The design resonated strongly enough to become canon rather than remaining an isolated promo concept: **Mara Reed is now the official recurring face/mascot of Rustwater.** Preserve that status unless the product deliberately changes direction.

She is **not the player character** and should never imply that every player canonically plays as Mara. Rustwater is an MMO in which players have their own persistent identity. Mara gives the game a recognizable human face and can serve as a visual window into different systems/regions without becoming the whole premise.

## Character concept

Mara is an adult human woman, approximately **22–25 years old**.

Personality anchors:

- curious;
- observant;
- self-reliant;
- quietly competitive;
- easygoing harbor-town confidence;
- expressive and grounded rather than bubbly/caricatured;
- visibly excited by unusual catches, forgotten places, strange weather, relics and unexplained discoveries.

She should feel capable of both ordinary harbor work and stepping into a much larger adventure.

## Tone

Default depiction: attractive, expressive, cute-to-neutral anime character design, but **not primarily sexualized/fanservice-oriented**.

A deliberately more suggestive promotional variant could exist if specifically requested for a particular asset, but that must not redefine her canonical design or become the default visual language.

Mara is human. Do not turn her into a nekomimi/elf/etc. as an incidental anime trope. Fantastical peoples may exist elsewhere in the eventual setting if the world design earns them.

## Recognition anchors

The three strongest instant-recognition anchors are:

1. **dark auburn/reddish-brown hair**;
2. **vivid teal-blue/blue-green eyes**;
3. **mustard/weathered yellow + deep navy maritime clothing**, especially the yellow jacket.

Keep these stable across normal variants.

### Physical design

- medium-long dark auburn / reddish-brown hair;
- roughly shoulder-blade length;
- slightly messy/windswept by coastal weather;
- recognizable loose side braid or partial side braid;
- vivid/deep teal-blue eyes;
- light-to-medium warm complexion;
- lean, healthy, outdoors-active build;
- expressive eyebrows;
- adventurous/capable face rather than exaggerated innocence.

## Signature palette

Primary:

- weathered mustard yellow;
- deep ocean/navy blue;
- muted teal.

Supporting:

- cream/off-white;
- weathered brown/leather;
- subtle rust/copper/brass accents.

The palette connects Mara to the sea/harbor while giving the game a recognizable warm visual accent.

## Signature outfit

The canonical outfit should read **“anime maritime adventurer”** rather than industrial fishing PPE, school uniform, maid outfit or generic fantasy armor.

Base outfit:

- cropped or waist-length mustard-yellow waterproof maritime jacket;
- deep navy/desaturated-blue inner layers;
- practical high-waisted trousers, or season-appropriate shorts+tights where suitable;
- weathered brown waterproof boots;
- utility belt / compact tackle pouches;
- practical or fingerless gloves when appropriate;
- optional navy scarf;
- believable wear from salt, rain and harbor work.

Practicality matters, but the silhouette should remain distinctive and appealing enough for key art.

## Iconic equipment/accessories

- distinctive fishing rod using dark graphite/wood tones with subtle brass fittings;
- **small Rusted Key/relic charm** attached to belt or satchel;
- compact weathered field journal;
- practical cross-body tackle satchel;
- occasional lantern for night/exploration scenes;
- subtle Rustwater emblem on a jacket, bag, rod case or pin when branding is useful.

The Rusted Key charm is especially useful because it communicates mystery/exploration beyond fishing.

Do **not** permanently associate Mara with one specific fish. Different catches/creatures should communicate the breadth of the world.

## Expression set

Useful canonical expression/pose directions:

- neutral / relaxed;
- excited after a rare catch;
- concentrated while fishing;
- curious / investigating;
- determined in bad weather;
- amused / social;
- surprised by a discovery;
- tired after an expedition.

## Outfit variants

Future controlled variants can include:

- rain gear;
- cold-weather layers;
- expedition clothing;
- casual harbor wear;
- festival/event outfit;
- region/environment-specific gear.

Variants should preserve the core recognition anchors unless the story intentionally changes her appearance.

## Use across the product

Mara can appear in:

- promotional key art;
- wallpapers;
- profile/avatar branding;
- NPC portraits;
- character sheets;
- location art;
- story/relationship scenes;
- event art;
- tutorial/onboarding imagery where an in-world guide is appropriate;
- social/promotional cards.

The environment should often carry nearly as much storytelling weight as Mara. A successful wallpaper should read as **Mara inside a living Rustwater world**, not “generic anime girl holding a fish.”

She can be shown fishing, exploring a lighthouse, crossing a stormy headland, inspecting a relic, watching a social harbor event, travelling to a new region, or eventually interacting with systems far beyond conventional fishing.

## Narrative relationship to the player

Current code presents Mara as Harbor Warden and progression-aware guide. Her deeper personal lore, relationship arc and exact long-term role remain to be authored.

Being the mascot does **not** automatically make her:

- the protagonist instead of the player;
- omnipresent in every story;
- romanceable;
- the source of every quest/system;
- immune to character development or consequences.

Use her prominence to strengthen recognition, not to shrink the world around her.
