# Open questions

These are intentionally unresolved. They are not a queue and should not be answered automatically just because a developer notices them. Resolve them when playtesting, concrete content, platform constraints, or a strong design need provides evidence.

## Product identity

- Is **Rustwater** ultimately the product title, the starting region, or both? It is currently the working game/world-thread name and the explicit starting place.
- How much of the enormous late-game setting remains maritime in visual/metaphorical language versus deliberately breaking away from it?

## Character model and specialization

- Should an account eventually support multiple characters, and would that enrich roleplay/specialization rather than merely let players bypass opportunity costs?
- What combination of traits, talents, professions, equipment investment, reputation, knowledge and social relationships best produces “no one can master everything” without generic stat inflation?
- Which specializations should be mutually exclusive, costly to reverse, or merely time-intensive?

## Narrative, reputation and relationships

- What is the exact reputation model: per NPC, faction, location/community, world organization, or a combination?
- How much dialogue is authored branching content versus compositional/state-driven variation?
- Which NPCs are romanceable? **Mara is not automatically romanceable merely because she is the mascot.**
- If romance exists, what are the expectations around exclusivity, consequences, breakups, alternate characters, and social visibility?
- How much relationship progress should affect gameplay capability versus story/access/cosmetic rewards?
- Should private-chat topics eventually map to NPC/story threads, or would that fragment the interface?

## Automation and asynchronous play

- How passive should activities become?
- Deployable nets/traps or expedition jobs sound compatible with short check-ins, but how far can automation go before it undermines discovery and active/social play?
- Could automation itself be a specialization rather than a universal optimal strategy?

## Geography and traversal

- When does directional navigation become valuable compared with destination-based travel buttons?
- Should long journeys be scheduled activities, map routes, vehicle interactions, encounters along a route, or a mixture?
- How do late-game speedups preserve geography and local identity?

## Economy and ownership

- When are player trading, auctions or markets worth introducing?
- How strongly should provenance, rarity, history, relationship ownership and visual variants affect collectability/value?
- Which resource sinks create choices rather than maintenance taxes?
- How, if ever, should Telegram Stars, collectible gifts, crypto/NFT representation or external ownership connect to in-world objects without becoming pay-to-win?

## Procedural content

- Which procedural specimen traits should influence behavior/value rather than remain visual?
- What rarity curves are appropriate for optional collectors?
- When are procedurally generated encounters, artifacts, locations or lore worth the validation/consistency burden?
- How should generator versions migrate if richer traits are added to old persistent specimens?

## Social world

- Which state belongs per player, chat, physical location, region, faction or globally?
- How can players alter shared environments without one Telegram chat destructively owning the canonical location for everybody?
- Which competition/cooperation patterns fit Telegram's public-message constraints best?
- What should a Rustwater “guild” or persistent social group mean relative to ordinary Telegram groups?

## Visual/generative media

- What becomes the final global illustration/rendering style? Current preference strongly favors polished anime/anime-inspired maritime fantasy, but only Mara's core visual anchors are truly canonical so far.
- Which images are generated offline and curated into content versus generated at runtime?
- How much variant art is stored per NPC/location/condition before asset volume becomes operationally expensive?
- Is the collectible gallery private, shareable, tradable, or partly social/world-historical?
- Can unlocked artwork itself have provenance/rarity, or should it remain a deterministic reward for accomplishments?

## Telegram surfaces

- What is the first classic inline-mode result set and query grammar?
- Which Guest Mode interactions feel magical enough to justify invoking Rustwater in arbitrary chats?
- Does a persistent reply keyboard ever beat Rich Message/inline navigation for a real encounter or accessibility use case?
- When does a Mini App become necessary for maps/inventory/markets/build planning rather than merely attractive?
- Would private-chat topics improve deep NPC/story organization or create more navigation burden?
