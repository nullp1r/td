# North star and MVP proof

## Locked product thesis

Rustwater is the working name of a starting-region concept, not necessarily the final game title. The target product is a **Telegram-native persistent sandbox MMO RPG** with one coherent shared world, meaningful character identity, social presence, exploration, items, trade, inhabitants, changing conditions and long-lived history.

Fishing is not privileged. It is one activity that may exist where water, tools, conditions and character capabilities make it possible. The architecture must not assume that the player is fundamentally a fisherman or that fish are the primary collectible/progression object.

The intended feeling is not “a large fishing bot.” It is that the player has entered a world that appears to contain far more places, objects, activities and consequences than the UI can immediately enumerate.

A useful social/emotional north star from the reset discussion is a player telling a friend that the Telegram interface feels unusually advanced, the world seems to contain effectively endless things to pursue, one attempted goal unexpectedly led to a rare object and a much larger thread, and that thread now gives them a concrete reason to ask the friend to join a party and help. The target is compulsion through curiosity, consequence and social need—not repetitive engagement mechanics for their own sake.

## Locked experiential loop

The core loop is:

```text
attention → curiosity → choice → anticipation → consequence → memory → new desire
```

Systems should create new reasons to act rather than merely award currency for repeating the previous action.

A desirable tonal expansion is:

```text
ordinary → curious → unusual → impossible → enormous
```

The starting region should remain human-scale and legible long enough for genuinely strange developments to retain impact.

## Leaning setting direction

The starting environment should feel familiar and human/Earth-like enough that the player can understand ordinary life, objects and social behavior before the world expands into stranger territory. Exact era, technology level, fantasy/supernatural rules and broader setting remain open.

The opening should not spend its mystery budget immediately. Ordinary outcomes must be allowed to remain ordinary so genuinely impossible events retain contrast.

## Locked “infinite world” strategy

The game should feel unbounded through composition rather than procedural adjective soup:

- authored nouns and places;
- reusable systemic verbs/capabilities;
- changing environmental/world conditions;
- unique item instances and provenance;
- character knowledge and relationships;
- player-created social/history consequences;
- combinations of those systems that create situations not individually scripted.

Reject content generation whose primary result is meaningless permutations such as “Legendary Rusted Fish of +3 Moisture.”

## Locked social premise

The world should be socially visible by default. Other characters matter through direct presence, recent presence, trade, records, discoveries, environmental consequences and durable history. Telegram group chats and inline mode are first-class acquisition and interaction surfaces, not merely notification channels.

A Telegram chat is not a world shard and is not a guild. The canonical world exists independently of the chat in which somebody encounters a shared object, profile, party or event.

## MVP proof

A serious MVP should demonstrate that one character can:

- enter the world as a real persistent character;
- occupy and move through a spatially coherent shared region;
- encounter several real NPCs with distinct roles/knowledge rather than one tutorial dispenser;
- discover multiple unrelated things to do;
- acquire both mundane and meaningful items;
- inspect enough context/provenance to understand why some objects matter;
- observe evidence of other real characters;
- inspect another character;
- give/trade something;
- experience changing canonical conditions;
- encounter at least one surprising non-script-loop outcome;
- share a canonical object/profile/event into another Telegram conversation;
- let another person interact with that shared surface;
- form or join a small cooperative objective.

The first content slice may contain only a handful of locations. Depth, recombination and social permeability matter more than map size.

## Rejected product shapes

- Fishing MMO with RPG decoration.
- Separate private copies of the world per player.
- Telegram-chat shards where the chat owns its own Rustwater.
- A static command/menu bot whose “world” is only navigation between screens.
- Repetition-first progression where the dominant strategy is spamming one action until a microscopic drop occurs.
- A tutorial that explains every system before the player is allowed into the actual world.
