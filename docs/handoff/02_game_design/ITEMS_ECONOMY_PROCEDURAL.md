# Items, Economy, Bait, and Procedural Content

> **Status:** settled principles, evolving systems

## Individual items and provenance

Fish/specimens are meaningful individual objects when their identity matters.

Important provenance may include:

- species;
- size/weight;
- catcher;
- location;
- time;
- bait;
- environmental snapshot;
- generator seed/version;
- future mutations/traits.

Selling, contract turn-in, or crafting should remove an item from usable inventory **without erasing its historical catch record**.

This supports records, trophies, trading, external representation, and world history later.

## Bait philosophy

Long term, “bait” is more a use/context than a rigid item category.

Potential properties:

- origin/material;
- freshness;
- smell;
- movement;
- size;
- elemental/magical properties;
- rarity;
- preparation;
- environmental compatibility.

The player-facing early UI should be much simpler than the underlying eventual system.

## Current prototype bait model

The prototype demonstrated:

- Fishing Power affecting bite wait;
- separate species attraction modifiers;
- crafted-only bait;
- material consumption from real specimens;
- possible positive/negative side-effect direction for future expansion.

## Economy

Early economy should remain understandable:

- one soft currency;
- sell catches;
- specimen size/value differences;
- buy meaningful gear/bait;
- low-friction repair;
- avoid irreversible traps/soft locks.

A dynamic market is not required for MVP.

## Procedural generation

Procedural generation is desirable when it produces meaningful collection/gameplay variation.

Early: deterministic size/weight/individual seed.

Future possibilities:

- morphology;
- coloration;
- mutations;
- traits;
- provenance modifiers;
- unusual effects;
- generated encounters/artifacts/locations.

Mandatory progression should not depend on uncontrolled extreme RNG. Optional collector outcomes can be far rarer.
