# Playtesting Guide

> **Status:** current process recommendation

## Highest-value test

Give the bot to a player with no live explanation and observe.

Do not rescue them immediately. Confusion is data.

## Questions to observe rather than ask

- Can they start?
- Do they understand where they are?
- Is the next action obvious?
- Do they notice conditions/gear/bait without reading a manual?
- When something bites, do they understand timing from the presentation?
- Can they recover after bait runs out?
- Can they navigate back from a deep screen?
- Do they discover/explore voluntarily?
- Do they care about a specimen/record?
- In a group, will they press the shared button without being told it is safe?
- Do they understand which result is private vs public?

## Qualitative success signals

- “What else can I catch?”
- “Does this bait change something?”
- “Why did that appear in fog?”
- “I want to beat that record.”
- “What is behind that location?”
- players discussing theories in the group.

## UX failure signals

- asking what `/game`/a screen even is;
- staring at an action because consequences are unclear;
- treating `/help` as something to ignore because it is too long;
- navigating through slash commands because buttons are insufficient;
- assuming social interactions must move to DM;
- accidental soft lock after consumables run out.

## Balance testing

Fine balance values should be tuned only after the interface clearly communicates the underlying choice. A confusing bait/rod UI makes economy feedback meaningless.
