# Product Vision

> **Status:** settled direction

## North star

Build a persistent, Telegram-native sandbox MMO RPG in which players explore a vast coherent world, discover systems and content, specialize over time, collect meaningful individual objects, interact socially, and always have another layer to pursue.

Fishing is the first deep activity, not the product definition.

A useful late-game thought experiment is intentionally absurd: a veteran player may eventually travel to the edge of the universe in a specialized vehicle and use a strange device to “fish” an entity from an alien/dimensional environment. If the foundational systems can evolve naturally toward that without the early game becoming abstract nonsense, the direction is healthy.

## What the game should feel like

- **Terraria:** discovery, item interactions, progression into bizarre environments, fishing as a surprisingly systemic activity.
- **World of Warcraft:** social world, specialization, long-lived character identity, layered progression, real-world event cadence paired with an in-world clock.
- **Skyrim/Fallout/LoL traits/talents:** inspiration for future nontrivial character differentiation.
- **Cookie Clicker:** only as inspiration for unbounded depth/long horizons, not for replacing active gameplay with idle automation.

## The emotional loop

The strongest player reaction should be:

> “I want to see what else is in here.”

Not:

> “This bot works.”

Curiosity should usually appear before explanation.

## Time horizons

The game should remain meaningful at:

- one action;
- one session;
- one evening;
- one week-long pursuit;
- one seasonal/month-long project;
- multi-year collection/social goals.

A ten-second interaction and a year-long goal should operate on the same persistent character/world rather than feeling like separate modes.

## World scope

The initial Rustwater region is a **small starting geography**, not the whole game and not necessarily the final game title.

Future scope may include:

- larger regions and continents;
- vertical/underground/underwater layers;
- dimensions;
- hostile environments;
- civilizations/factions;
- vehicles;
- infrastructure;
- supernatural/technological fishing equivalents;
- per-chat/local/global events;
- player environmental influence.

## Product surfaces

The world should be accessible through multiple cohesive clients:

- Telegram DMs;
- Telegram group chats;
- ephemeral per-user group views;
- Telegram Rich Messages;
- future Mini App;
- future website/other interfaces.

All are surfaces onto one authoritative world rather than independent games.
